_Films and Games to Which I’ve Contributed_


I work where **creative work, business operations, people, and technology meet.** I design workflows and the architecture behind them, connecting how work actually gets done with where AI can genuinely help.

At **Walt Disney Animation Studios**, **Weta Digital**, **Sony Pictures Imageworks**, **Digital Domain**, and **EA**, I helped shape productions that pushed the industry forward. From *Tangled* and *Frozen* to *Zootopia* and *Moana*, my focus has always been the same: **merge artistic vision with technical precision** so every frame resonates.

That started in production. At **Walt Disney Animation Studios**, **Weta Digital**, **Sony Pictures Imageworks**, **Digital Domain**, and **EA**, I built the pipelines and tools behind films like Tangled, Frozen, Zootopia, and Moana.

Then I took it into education. As **Chair of one of the world's top animation programs**, I built a student-run studio, produced original films, and mentored the next wave of artists and technologists.

> **Now I do it for businesses and creative teams** bringing AI into their work, across customer and user success, operations, and knowledge systems. What that looks like depends on who I'm working with and what they need.

**With some teams, the work is AI enablement.** They have the tools but not the habits. I help them find where AI is worth using, train people on it, and set the guardrails that make it part of the daily routine.

With others, it's **AI architecture.** They know what they want and need it built to last. I design the workflow and the system behind it: where the data comes from, where a person reviews the output, who makes the decision, and how it holds up past the prototype.

Often it's both. A well-designed system needs people who trust it, and a willing team needs something dependable to use.

**Either way, the throughline is friction, and most of it is invisible.** It's the file an animator has to hunt for, the handoff that drops a detail, the report someone rebuilds by hand every month. People stop noticing it and work around it.

I map how the work gets done, then design the architecture that takes the friction out:
- **Artists** stay in the shot instead of fighting the pipeline.
- **Technologists** build on a system that's documented and repeatable.
- **Students** learn how a studio runs by working inside one.
- **Customer success teams** see what needs attention today without digging through several systems.

When it works, nobody notices the tool. They just get to focus on the work.
